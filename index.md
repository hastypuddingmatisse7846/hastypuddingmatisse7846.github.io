## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is an `.ipa` file?
An `.ipa` file is an iOS app package. It's like a `.exe` file for Windows, but made for iPhones and iPads.

### Do I need to jailbreak my iPhone?
**No.** iloader works without jailbreaking. It uses standard Apple methods for sideloading.

### My app won't open after installing. What do I do?
Make sure you completed the "Trust" step (Step 4 above). Also, check that your iOS device is connected to the internet the first time you open the sideloaded app.

### Can I install apps from any source?
You can install apps from `.ipa` files that you obtain legally. Make sure you trust the source of the app file.

### How many apps can I install?
That depends on your iOS setup. With a free Apple ID, you can sign apps for 7 days. With a paid developer account, you can sign apps for a year. iloader will show you the status.

### Is iloader safe?
Yes, iloader is built with safety in mind. It does not modify your device's system files.

## 📚 Troubleshooting

### Problem: "Device Not Found"
- Make sure your USB cable is working.
- Try a different USB port on your computer.
- Unlock your iPhone and check if you tapped "Trust This Computer".

### Problem: Installation Gets Stuck
- Check your internet connection.
- Disconnect and reconnect your device.
- Close and reopen iloader, then try again.

### Problem: App Closes Immediately After Opening
- Reinstall the app using iloader.
- Make sure your iOS version is compatible (iOS 14 or later).
